Relational Database & SQL Console

Visual Entity-Relationship representation of the 6 tables with live multi-table JOIN query execution.

Entity-Relationship Architecture 6 Tables & Foreign Keys

TABLE: CUSTOMER Master
🔑 CUSTOMER_ID NUMBER (PK)
NAME VARCHAR2(30)
PHONE VARCHAR2(15)
EMAIL VARCHAR2(50)
ADDRESS VARCHAR2(50)
TABLE: EMPLOYEE Master
🔑 EMPLOYEE_ID NUMBER (PK)
EMPLOYEE_NAME VARCHAR2(50)
DEPARTMENT VARCHAR2(50)
PHONE VARCHAR2(15)
EMAIL VARCHAR2(50)
TABLE: PRODUCT Master
🔑 PRODUCT_ID NUMBER (PK)
PRODUCT_NAME VARCHAR2(30)
CATEGORY VARCHAR2(30)
PRICE NUMBER(10,2)
STOCK NUMBER
TABLE: PAYMENT Master
🔑 PAYMENT_ID NUMBER (PK)
AMOUNT NUMBER(10,2)
PAYMENT_DATE DATE
PAYMENT_METHOD VARCHAR2(30)
PAYMENT_STATUS VARCHAR2(30)
TABLE: ORDERS Central Relational Hub
🔑 BOOKING_ID NUMBER (PK)
ORDER_DATE DATE
QUANTITY NUMBER
TOTAL_AMOUNT NUMBER(10,2)
🔗 CUSTOMER_ID FK → CUSTOMER
🔗 EMPLOYEE_ID FK → EMPLOYEE
🔗 PRODUCT_ID FK → PRODUCT
🔗 PAYMENT_ID FK → PAYMENT
TABLE: SUPPORT Service
🔑 TICKET_ID NUMBER (PK)
ISSUE VARCHAR2(100)
STATUS VARCHAR2(30)
OPEN_DATE DATE
🔗 CUSTOMER_ID FK → CUSTOMER
